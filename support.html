Getting started

Open the app and answer a few questions about your sport, experience, schedule, and goals. Your training plan generates automatically — no account setup required.

Saving your progress

Your plan lives on your device until you connect a Google or Apple account from the Account tab. Once connected, your training data follows you to a new phone or after reinstalling.

Adjusting your plan

Ask the coach to lighten a day, move a session, or rework the week. The coach proposes the change and you approve it before anything updates.

Elevate Pro

Pro unlocks unlimited plan generation, unlimited coaching chat, and full plan adjustments. Available monthly or annually.

Managing your subscription

Subscriptions renew automatically and are managed through your app store account, not through us. To cancel or change your plan:

iPhone: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions
Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstalled or switched devices, sign in with the same account you subscribed with and your Pro access will restore automatically. If it doesn't, email us.

Deleting your account

Email us and we will delete your account and all associated training data within 30 days.
